Pega Visão Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026. In case of any divergence between versions, the Portuguese version prevails.

PTENES中文

1. Who is responsible

The Pega Visão app is operated by JORGE VICTOR DA SILVA BRAGA PORTUGAL DESENVOLVIMENTO DE SOFTWARE LTDA ("TEVOX"), registered under CNPJ 68.512.157/0001-30, which is the controller of the data described here. Contact for questions and requests about your data: suporte@tevox.co.

2. What the app does

Pega Visão helps people moving around Rio de Janeiro with information about areas (Free movement, Caution, Local guide required), real-time reports from other people, events, tourist spots and restaurants. It was built to also work without internet: the content is downloaded and stored on your device.

3. Data we process and why

  • Anonymous account. When you open the app, it automatically creates an anonymous account on our server. We do not ask for a name, email, phone number or password. The account is identified only by a random code, and we store with it the language, the creation date and an internal trust score (used to decide how your reports are published, see item 5).
  • Location. See item 4.
  • Safety reports. See item 5.
  • Purchases (Travel Pass). See item 6.
  • Data stored on your device. Downloaded content (areas, reports, events, places, itineraries), reports not yet sent and the state of your Pass are kept in an encrypted local database (SQLCipher); the encryption key is kept in the system's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). Some simple home-screen preferences are kept in the app's local storage. This data is not sent to us, except what is described in items 4 to 6.

As far as the code shows, the app does not contain third-party advertising, usage analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and we do not sell or rent data. [CONFIRMAR before each store submission, if any SDK of this kind is added.]

4. Location

  • The app only uses location while it is open (foreground). It does not request or use background location; on Android the background location permission is explicitly removed.
  • Location is requested when you tap to center the map on yourself and when you are about to submit a report. If you deny the permission, the app keeps working, without those two features.
  • To center the map, your position is used only on your device.
  • Your position only leaves the device when you submit a safety report: in that case we send the coordinates (latitude and longitude) captured at that moment. The app's content update sends the server only the name of the content package (for example, "tourist circuit" or "Full Rio"), not your position.
  • The map is displayed by Google Maps (see item 7), which may process technical data from your device according to Google's policy.

5. Safety reports

You can report an incident in one of four categories: risk of confrontation, police action, blocked road or street event. Each report stores: the category, the coordinates of the location, the code of your anonymous account (as author), the status of the report, the number of confirmations and rejections from other people, and the creation and expiration dates. There is no free-text field, photo or audio.

Reports serve to alert other people and expire automatically (from 3 to 12 hours, depending on the category); once expired, they stop appearing on the map. Reports not yet sent stay in a queue on your device, with the capture time, and are sent when there is a connection. The server decides whether the report is published immediately or stays pending, based on the account's trust score. Published reports are shown to other users without your account code.

6. Purchases and Travel Pass

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive or store card data, payment name or billing address.
  • To unlock the Pass, the app sends our server the purchase receipt provided by the store (on Google Play, the purchase token; on the App Store, the transaction identifier). The server validates this receipt directly with the store.
  • What we store: the code of your anonymous account, the originating store, the subscription and transaction identifiers in the store, and the date the paid period ends. The store also notifies us of renewals, cancellations and refunds, to keep the Pass up to date.
  • Because the account is anonymous, the Pass is tied to it and to your device/store account; use "Restore purchase" on the Pass screen when changing devices. The Pass is a monthly subscription with automatic renewal, charged by the store; you can cancel at any time in your Google Play or App Store subscriptions, and access continues until the end of the period already paid.

  • Google Cloud Translation — used on our server to translate only event content (title and description) obtained from a public Rio cultural agenda source. No user data, location or report is sent to this service.
