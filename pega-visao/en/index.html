How to use it in three steps

  1. Get informed before you leave

    Open the map, check the areas along your way and plan your day calmly.

  2. Stay in the loop on the street

    Community reports flag a blocked road, police action, a street event or a risk. They are short-lived and clear on their own.

  3. Enjoy the city

    Pick an event, a restaurant or a museum. No signal? Data you have already downloaded stays available.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to speak Portuguese?

No. The whole app works in English, Portuguese, Spanish and Simplified Chinese. You choose the language in your Profile.

Does the map tell me a place is safe?

No. It helps you plan your day, but it does not guarantee the safety of any route. Use it along with your own judgment and local advice.

Will the app be paid?

Yes. When it launches, Pega Visão will work with a monthly subscription, the Travel Pass, which unlocks the whole app. The store will show the price in your local currency, and you will be able to cancel any time.

Does it work without internet?

Data you have already downloaded stays available when the signal drops. The map drawing itself needs internet.

What are reports?

Notices sent by people on the street: a blocked road, police action, a street event or a risk. They are short-lived, clear from the map on their own and may be incomplete.

When can I download it?

Not yet: Pega Visão is under construction and has no launch date set. Once it is on Google Play and the App Store, the download buttons will appear here.
