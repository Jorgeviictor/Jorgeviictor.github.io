3. What the app offers

For people moving around the covered regions of the State of Rio de Janeiro, Pega Visão brings together information about map areas (“Free circulation”, “Caution”, “Local guide required”), safety reports sent by other people, events, tourist attractions, restaurants and itineraries. Part of the content is downloaded and stored on the device for offline use. The app works with an anonymous account, created automatically, with no name, e-mail or phone number. The safety features (areas, reports and map) are free.

  • The “Caution” label marks mapped community areas. It is a guidance label, not a crime index or a judgement about the people who live or work there. The classification has not yet been reviewed zone by zone and may change; if you spot a mistake, write to support and tell us which zone it is.

5. Reports sent by users

  • How they work. A report is chosen from a fixed list of categories (for example, “Blocked road”, “Street event”, “Police action”, “Risk or confrontation”), with no free text, photo or audio, and is tied to the place where you are. An automatic server rule decides whether a report is published immediately or stays pending, based on the account’s trust level: accounts at the highest level publish immediately; at the other levels the report stays pending, is not shown to other people and expires at the normal time. The levels and criteria are in the Privacy Policy, and you can ask for a person to review that decision (Brazilian General Data Protection Law, article 20).
